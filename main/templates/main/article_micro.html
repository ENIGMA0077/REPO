🦠 Micro World: Life Hidden in Plain Sight

The invisible foundation of all ecosystems

What is the Micro World?

The micro world includes all life forms too small to see without a microscope. Despite their size, they are essential for life on Earth.

Insects

Insects are the most diverse group of animals on Earth.

  • Pollinate plants
  • Recycle organic matter
  • Support food chains

Bees

Bees are critical for pollination and food production.

  • Support agriculture
  • Maintain biodiversity
  • Enable plant reproduction

Bacteria

Bacteria exist everywhere — soil, water, air and inside organisms.

  • Break down organic matter
  • Support digestion
  • Produce oxygen in oceans

Mollusks

Includes snails, squids, octopuses and clams.

  • Some are highly intelligent (octopus)
  • Important for marine ecosystems

Conclusion

The micro world is the invisible engine of life on Earth.
