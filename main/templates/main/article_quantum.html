⚛ Quantum Physics: The Science of the Very Small World

Where reality stops being certain and becomes probability

What is Quantum Physics?

Quantum physics explains how the universe behaves at the smallest scale — atoms, electrons, photons and subatomic particles.

Unlike classical physics, quantum physics does not describe exact outcomes, but probabilities.

Why It Matters

  • Computers and smartphones rely on quantum effects
  • Lasers and LEDs exist because of quantum theory
  • Medical technologies like MRI depend on quantum physics

The Strange Quantum World

1. Wave-Particle Duality

Particles like electrons behave both like waves and particles.

2. Superposition

A particle can exist in multiple states at once until observed.

3. Uncertainty Principle

We cannot know position and velocity of a particle at the same time.

Famous Experiments

  • Double-slit experiment
  • Quantum entanglement tests

Conclusion

Quantum physics shows that reality is not fixed — it is probabilistic and strange beyond intuition.
