🐘 Macro World: Giants and Ecosystems

The visible scale of nature and its largest systems

What is the Macro World?

The macro world includes everything we can see without tools: large animals, oceans, forests, and ecosystems.

Giant Animals

  • Elephants
  • Whales
  • Giraffes
  • Rhinos

These animals shape ecosystems by controlling vegetation and maintaining balance.

Predators

  • Lions
  • Sharks
  • Wolves
  • Eagles

Predators control population and prevent ecosystem collapse.

The Ocean

The ocean covers over 70% of Earth’s surface.

  • Home to whales and sharks
  • Regulates climate
  • Produces oxygen via plankton

Ecosystems

  • Rainforests
  • Savannas
  • Oceans
  • Tundras

Everything is connected in a global system.

Conclusion

The macro world shows the scale and power of nature.
